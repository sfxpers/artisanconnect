# Which documents evidence a current permission to work?

Researched 24 September 2026. National South African law. Primary sources only: the statute, the Constitutional Court order that now reads into it, and the Department of Home Affairs' own regulations, gazettes, directives, and forms.

This note does not pick a vendor, and it does not draft product terms. It does not reopen the decision in [What must be true before an Artisan can Quote, and what does a badge prove?](https://github.com/sfxpers/artisanconnect/issues/64): a permission to work is a silent gate, not a Verification Badge. A South African ID, and a recognised refugee identity document, need no second check. This note is only about an Artisan whose Identity Number is a passport.

## How to read the answer

The Immigration Act defines "work" as conducting any activity normally associated with running a specific business, or being employed or conducting activities consistent with being employed, with or without remuneration, inside the Republic. [Immigration Act 13 of 2002, s 1, definition of "work"](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf). Marketplace labour is work on that definition.

"Issued to the person" means the instrument names the foreigner. "Tied" means the work it allows is only for a named employer, a named business, a named programme, or a stated activity. A tied permission is not a permission to take any other work.

A visa is issued on condition that the holder is not, and does not become, a prohibited or undesirable person. The Director-General may attach reasonable terms to a visa. The holder's status expires if those terms are broken, and the holder must depart when the status expires. [Immigration Act, ss 10(4), 10(5), 43](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf). No person may employ a foreigner whose status does not authorise employment by that person, or employ them in a different capacity. [Immigration Act, s 38(1)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf).

The work-visa sections below are from the Department's consolidation of the Act, marked "26 May 2014 – to date". The only later Act on the government's amendment list is the [Immigration Amendment Act 8 of 2016](https://www.gov.za/documents/immigration-amendment-act-8-2016-english-siswati-27-sep-2016-0000), which provides a sanction for overstaying. It does not amend the work sections. The government's [Immigration Act page](https://www.gov.za/documents/immigration-act) lists no amendment after that Act.

Visas are issued by the Director-General of Home Affairs, unless a section below names someone else. [Immigration Act, s 10](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf).

## What evidences a current permission

A passport alone does not. The permission is a separate instrument, and it evidences a current permission only while it is in force and only for the work it actually allows.

### Permanent residence permit

Issued by the Director-General under sections 25, 26, or 27. [Immigration Act, ss 25–27](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf).

The holder has all the rights, privileges, duties, and obligations of a citizen, except those a law or the Constitution explicitly ascribes to citizenship. [Immigration Act, s 25(1)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf). The Act's prohibitions on conducting work are attached to named temporary visas, not to this permit. The Act treats a permanent resident as part of the local labour pool that a work visa must not displace. [Immigration Act, s 15(1)(c)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf); [Immigration Regulations, 2014, reg 18(3)](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/ImmigrationRegulations2014-Updated2018-compressed.pdf).

It attaches to the person. It does not expire by date. It can lapse or be withdrawn: a spousal permit lapses if the good-faith relationship ends within two years, other than by death; a business permit lapses if the prescribed investment is not still in place at the stated checks; the Director-General may withdraw a permit for a listed conviction, breach of its conditions, absence of more than three years, or failure to take up residence within a year. [Immigration Act, ss 26(b), 27(c)(ii), 28](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf). A permit issued on an offer of permanent employment is issued on condition that the holder remains employed for five years in the field of that offer. [Immigration Regulations, reg 24(2)](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/ImmigrationRegulations2014-Updated2018-compressed.pdf).

### General work visa

Issued by the Director-General under section 19(2) to a foreigner who is not in the critical-skills category and who meets the prescribed requirements. [Immigration Act, s 19(2)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf).

It is issued to the person, but the work is tied to the employment contract. The application must include that contract, and the employer must tell the Director-General when the person is no longer employed, or is employed in a different capacity or role. [Immigration Regulations, reg 18(3)](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/ImmigrationRegulations2014-Updated2018-compressed.pdf). It may be issued for a period not exceeding five years. [Immigration Regulations, reg 18(4)](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/ImmigrationRegulations2014-Updated2018-compressed.pdf). It expires.

From 9 October 2024, an application must be adjudicated on the points-based system the Minister determines. [Third Amendment of the Immigration Regulations, 2014, GN 5398, GG 51366, 9 October 2024, reg 18(12)](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/notices/8october24/Gazette-Third-Amendment-of-the-Immigration-Regulations-2014.pdf). An offer of employment is labelled mandatory. Reaching 100 points other than through an occupation on the critical skills list, and meeting the other prescribed requirements, is what yields a general work visa. [GN 5448, GG 51416, 18 October 2024](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202410/51416gon5448.pdf).

It does not evidence permission to work for anyone other than the employer, or in any capacity other than the one, in that contract.

### Critical skills work visa

Issued by the Director-General under section 19(4) to a person who has skills or qualifications the Minister has determined, by notice in the Gazette, to be critical. [Immigration Act, s 19(4)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf).

It is issued to the person. Unlike the intra-company transfer visa, section 19 does not say the holder may work only for one named employer. [Immigration Act, s 19(6)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf), which confines only the intra-company transfer. An offer of employment is nevertheless a mandatory part of the points-based adjudication, and 100 points reached through an occupation on the critical skills list is what yields this visa rather than a general work visa. [GN 5448, GG 51416](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202410/51416gon5448.pdf). The visa may still carry conditions. [Immigration Act, s 10(5)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf).

It may be issued for a period not exceeding five years. [Immigration Regulations, reg 18(6)](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/ImmigrationRegulations2014-Updated2018-compressed.pdf). It expires. If the applicant has only applied for a required professional registration, it may be issued for not more than 12 months. [Second Amendment of the Immigration Regulations, 2014, GN R4847, GG 50675, 20 May 2024](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202405/50675rg11704gon4847.pdf), amending reg 18(5).

### Intra-company transfer work visa

Issued by the Director-General under section 19(5). The holder may conduct work only for the employer referred to in that subsection, and only as the visa requires. [Immigration Act, s 19(5) and (6)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf). The employer must employ the foreigner only in the specific position for which the visa was issued. [Immigration Regulations, reg 18(9)](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/ImmigrationRegulations2014-Updated2018-compressed.pdf).

It is issued to the person, and the work is tied to that employer and position. It may be issued for a period not exceeding four years, and it is not renewable. [Immigration Regulations, reg 18(10)](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/ImmigrationRegulations2014-Updated2018-compressed.pdf). It expires.

### Corporate worker certificate

The corporate visa itself is issued to a corporate applicant, which is a juristic person, not to the worker. It lets that applicant employ foreigners who may conduct work for it. [Immigration Act, s 21(1), and s 1, definition of "corporate applicant"](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf). A foreigner employed under it shall work for the holder of that corporate visa. [Immigration Act, s 21(6)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf).

The document the person holds is the corporate worker certificate, issued by the Director-General, for a period not exceeding the corporate visa. The corporate visa itself may not exceed three years. [Immigration Regulations, reg 20(8) and (10)](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/ImmigrationRegulations2014-Updated2018-compressed.pdf). The certificate expires. The worker may not renew it, or apply for a change of status, in the Republic. [Immigration Regulations, reg 20(11)](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/ImmigrationRegulations2014-Updated2018-compressed.pdf).

The certificate is issued to the person. The permission does not attach as a personal freedom to work. It is held through the corporate applicant, and only for that applicant, in the specific position. [Immigration Regulations, reg 20(5)(b)](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/ImmigrationRegulations2014-Updated2018-compressed.pdf).

### Business visa

Issued by the Director-General under section 15 to a foreigner who is establishing or investing in, or who has established or invested in, a business in the Republic in which he or she may be employed. The holder may not conduct work other than work related to the business for which the visa was issued. [Immigration Act, s 15(1) and (2)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf).

It is issued to the person, and the work is tied to that business. It may be issued for a period not exceeding three years at a time. [Immigration Regulations, reg 14(5)](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/ImmigrationRegulations2014-Updated2018-compressed.pdf). It expires, and it may be renewed. The holder must prove the investment requirements within 24 months, and every two years after that. [Immigration Act, s 15(4)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf).

The Department's 2024/25 annual report describes a start-up route as part of the business visa, not as a separate visa. No separate start-up instrument appears in the Second or Third Amendment of the Immigration Regulations, 2014. This note does not treat a start-up visa as its own instrument.

### Exchange visa for work

Issued by the Director-General under section 22(b) to a foreigner under 25 who has an offer to conduct work for no longer than one year. The foreigner may not conduct work other than the work for which the visa is issued. [Immigration Act, s 22(b)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf). It may be issued for a period not exceeding the exchange programme. [Immigration Regulations, reg 21(5)](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/ImmigrationRegulations2014-Updated2018-compressed.pdf).

It is issued to the person, and the work is tied to that offer. It expires. A programme visa under section 22(a) authorises the programme's activities, not work at large. The organ of state or learning institution must describe those activities. [Immigration Act, s 22(a)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf); [Immigration Regulations, reg 21](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/ImmigrationRegulations2014-Updated2018-compressed.pdf).

### Visitor's visa with a work authorisation endorsed on it

The holder of a visitor's visa may not conduct work, unless the visa was issued under section 11(1)(a) or 11(1)(b)(iv) and the Director-General has authorised work, in the prescribed manner, on prescribed terms. [Immigration Act, s 11(2)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf). If approved, the authorisation is endorsed on the visa. It may not be extended beyond the validity of a section 11(1)(a) visa. [Immigration Regulations, reg 11(7)](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/ImmigrationRegulations2014-Updated2018-compressed.pdf).

A section 11(1)(a) visa may not exceed three months, and a renewal may not exceed a further three months. A section 11(1)(b) visa may not exceed three years. [Immigration Act, s 11(1)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf).

The endorsement is issued to the person. The work is tied to the purpose, nature, place, duration, and prospective employer stated in the application. [Immigration Regulations, reg 11(7)(a)](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/ImmigrationRegulations2014-Updated2018-compressed.pdf). A visitor's visa with no endorsement does not qualify. See below.

A remote-work endorsement is one of those authorisations, and it does not qualify for local work. The prescribed activity is work for a foreign employer on a remote basis, at no less than the equivalent of R650,976 a year. [Third Amendment, GN 5398, substituting reg 11(4)(b)](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/notices/8october24/Gazette-Third-Amendment-of-the-Immigration-Regulations-2014.pdf). The other prescribed activities in regulation 11(4)(a) are likewise work for a foreign employer in listed roles, such as an international-school teacher, a film crew member, or a visiting academic. [GN R4847, reg 11(4)(a)](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202405/50675rg11704gon4847.pdf). None of those is a permission to work as an artisan for a client in the Republic.

### Relative's visa, only for one class of holder

The Act says the holder of a relative's visa may not conduct work. [Immigration Act, s 18(2)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf).

That prohibition no longer covers one class. In *Rayment and Others v Minister of Home Affairs and Others; Anderson and Others v Minister of Home Affairs and Others* [2023] ZACC 40 (4 December 2023), the Constitutional Court declared section 18(2) invalid to the extent that it does not allow a foreigner, otherwise eligible for a relative's visa, to work where that foreigner is the parent of a child who is a citizen or a permanent resident, and is fulfilling, or demonstrates an intention to fulfil, parental responsibilities to that child. During a 24-month suspension, section 18(2) was deemed to read that such a parent "shall be allowed to work in the Republic for the full duration of the visa." The order says the reading-in becomes final if Parliament does not correct the defect within 24 months of the judgment. [Order, paras 3.3, 5.2, and 6](https://www.concourt.org.za/images/Final_judgment_CCT_176-22_Rayment_v_Ministers_of_Home_Affairs_Anderson_v_Minister_of_Home_Affairs-1.pdf). That period ended on 4 December 2025. The government's Act page, read on 24 September 2026, lists no amendment after the Immigration Amendment Act 8 of 2016. On those sources, the reading-in is the text in force.

The visa is issued by the Director-General to the person. [Immigration Act, s 18(1)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf). For that parent, the work permission attaches to the person for the full duration of the visa. It is not tied to a named employer. The visa may be issued for a maximum of two years at a time. [Immigration Regulations, reg 17(3)](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/ImmigrationRegulations2014-Updated2018-compressed.pdf). It expires. A relative's visa held by anyone else still may not be used to work.

### Zimbabwean Exemption Permit and Lesotho Exemption Permit

These are exemptions the Minister of Home Affairs grants under section 31(2)(b), which lets the Minister grant a foreigner, or a category of foreigners, the rights of permanent residence for a specified or unspecified period when special circumstances exist. [Immigration Act, s 31(2)(b)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf).

The Minister's conditions for the permits issued from the 2023 decision are that the holder is entitled to work, seek employment, and conduct business in the Republic. The same decision says the holder is not entitled to apply for permanent residence under sections 25, 26, or 27, and cannot change status in the Republic under section 10(6). [Department of Home Affairs press statement on the granting of exemptions to Lesotho and Zimbabwean nationals, 1 December 2023, paras 5.1, 5.5, 5.8, 6.1, 6.5, and 6.8](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/LSP/Lesotho-Directive-No3-of-2023.pdf). Those permits were to expire on 29 November 2025.

On 7 October 2025 the Minister extended existing permits, again under section 31(2)(b), until 28 May 2027. Existing permits are deemed to remain valid for 18 months after the then expiry date of 28 November 2025. [GN R6716, GG 53484, 7 October 2025](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/notices/53484-7-10-ZEP-Notice.pdf) (Zimbabwean); [GN R6715, GG 53483, 7 October 2025](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/notices/53483-7-10-LEP-Notice.pdf) (Lesotho). The extension continues the exemption. It does not withdraw the work condition.

The permit is issued to the person. The work entitlement is not tied to a named employer. It expires. On 24 September 2026 the extended date is 28 May 2027. A permit the Minister has withdrawn, or that was never issued, is not evidence.

### Study visa, only within the prescribed limit

The holder of a study visa may conduct certain work as prescribed. [Immigration Act, s 13(2)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf). The prescription is narrow: the holder of a study visa at a higher-education institution, or at a further-education and training college, may conduct part-time work for not more than 20 hours a week. [Immigration Regulations, reg 12(3)](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/ImmigrationRegulations2014-Updated2018-compressed.pdf). A school study visa has no such provision.

It is issued to the person. The permission is the 20-hour part-time limit, not a permission to work as an artisan at large. The visa is issued for the duration of the course, and it lapses if the holder fails to register or is deregistered. [Immigration Regulations, reg 12(2) and (4)](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/ImmigrationRegulations2014-Updated2018-compressed.pdf). It expires.

### Asylum seeker visa, only if a right to work is endorsed and still current

An asylum seeker is not, as such, entitled to seek employment. Section 27A lists the asylum seeker's rights. Seeking employment is not among them. Seeking employment is a right of a recognised refugee, under section 27(f), which is why a recognised refugee identity document is outside this question. [Refugees Act 130 of 1998, ss 27(f) and 27A](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/REFUGEES_ACT_130_OF_1998.pdf), as substituted and inserted with effect from 1 January 2020.

The asylum seeker visa allows the holder to sojourn temporarily, subject to conditions. [Refugees Act, s 22(1)](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201712/refugeesamendmentact-11-of2017g41343gon1452.pdf), as substituted by section 18 of the Refugees Amendment Act 11 of 2017, in force from 1 January 2020. The right to work may not be endorsed in the cases section 22(8) lists. If it is endorsed, the employer must give the Department a letter of employment within 14 days of the asylum seeker taking up employment. The Director-General must revoke the endorsed right if the holder cannot prove employment six months after the endorsement. [Refugees Act, s 22(8), (9), and (11)](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201712/refugeesamendmentact-11-of2017g41343gon1452.pdf).

The Department's own form makes the split express. Condition 4 is printed as alternatives: "The visa entitles the holder to seek employment" or "The visa does not entitle the holder to work in the Republic." [Refugees Regulations, 2018, Form 5, GN 1707, GG 42932, 27 December 2019](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202001/42932rg11024gon1707.pdf). Before any right to seek employment is issued, the section 22(6) assessment must be completed. [Refugees Regulations, reg 12(5)](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202001/42932rg11024gon1707.pdf).

An asylum seeker visa with no work endorsement does not qualify. One with a current endorsement evidences a permission to seek employment, issued to the person, until the visa expires or the endorsement is revoked. The visa may be extended while the asylum application is pending. [Refugees Act, s 22(4)](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201712/refugeesamendmentact-11-of2017g41343gon1452.pdf). It is not a general, untied, non-expiring permission.

### Retired person visa, only if the Director-General has authorised work

A retired person visa does not itself allow work. The Director-General may authorise the holder to conduct work on terms the Director-General determines. [Immigration Act, s 20(2)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf). Without that authorisation, the visa does not qualify. With it, the permission is whatever those terms say. The visa may not exceed four years, and it may be renewed. [Immigration Act, s 20(3)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf). It expires. It is issued to the person.

### A spouse's visitor's visa, only if work has been authorised on it

A visitor's visa may be issued under section 11(6) to the spouse of a citizen or permanent resident who does not qualify for a visa under sections 13 to 22. On application, the holder may be authorised to perform any of the activities those visas provide for. [Immigration Act, s 11(6)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf). The visa alone is not an authorisation to work. The *Rayment* reading-in keeps such a visa from lapsing, for a parent of a citizen or permanent-resident child who is fulfilling or intends to fulfil parental responsibilities, pending an application for a new visa. [Order, para 5.1](https://www.concourt.org.za/images/Final_judgment_CCT_176-22_Rayment_v_Ministers_of_Home_Affairs_Anderson_v_Minister_of_Home_Affairs-1.pdf). That reading-in continues the visa. It does not itself confer work.

## What does not qualify

These were checked. None of them, by itself, evidences a current permission to work.

**Visitor's visa with no work endorsement.** The holder may not conduct work. [Immigration Act, s 11(2)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf). A port-of-entry visa, and an exemption from needing one in order to obtain a visitor's visa, are authorities to travel and to be admitted. They are not a permission to work. [Immigration Act, ss 10A(3) and 10A(4)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf).

**Relative's visa, other than the parent class above.** The holder may not conduct work. [Immigration Act, s 18(2)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf), as read in by *Rayment*.

**Asylum seeker visa with no work endorsement.** Section 27A does not entitle an asylum seeker to seek employment. The Department's form has a printed alternative that the visa does not entitle the holder to work. [Refugees Act, s 27A](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/REFUGEES_ACT_130_OF_1998.pdf); [Form 5, condition 4](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202001/42932rg11024gon1707.pdf).

**Asylum transit visa.** Valid for five days only, to travel to the nearest Refugee Reception Office. [Immigration Act, s 23(1)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf).

**Medical treatment visa.** It does not entitle the holder to conduct work. [Immigration Act, s 17(3)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf).

**Crew visa.** The holder may not conduct work. [Immigration Act, s 16(3)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf).

**Treaty visa, by itself.** It authorises activities under an international agreement, as the responsible organ of state describes them. It is not a permission to work at large. [Immigration Act, s 14](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf); [Immigration Regulations, reg 13](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/ImmigrationRegulations2014-Updated2018-compressed.pdf).

**A receipt for a pending visa, waiver, or appeal.** An application for a change of status does not provide a status, and does not entitle the applicant to sojourn pending the decision, except as the Act explicitly sets out. [Immigration Act, s 10(8)](https://www.dha.gov.za/IMMIGRATION_ACT_2002_MAY2014.pdf). Immigration Directive No. 7 of 2026 extends the current visa status of certain holders who have already applied for a long-term visa, until 30 June 2027, and says they may not engage in any activity other than what the current visa conditions provide for. It does not apply to a permanent-residence application. The concession ends when the outcome is received. [Immigration Directive No. 7 of 2026, 30 March 2026, paras 3.2.1, 3.3.1, 5, and 7](https://www.dha.gov.za/images/PDFs/Concession_of_30_March_2026.pdf). The directive can extend a permission the person already holds. The receipt is not itself a permission.

**MEETS.** The Department's Meetings, Events, Exhibitions and Tourism Scheme lets accredited event organisers submit group visa applications for international visitors to events. It is not an instrument that authorises an artisan to work. [Department of Home Affairs, MEETS](https://www.dha.gov.za/index.php/immigration-services/meets).

**Angolan special permit.** No current ministerial extension of an Angolan special permit was found in the Department notices retrieved for this note. It is not treated as a current instrument.

## What was not decided

This note names the instruments and the limits the sources put on them. It does not decide which of the tied permissions, if any, should satisfy the silent gate for an Artisan who quotes on the platform. A general work visa, an intra-company transfer visa, a corporate worker certificate, a business visa, an exchange visa, a visitor's-visa endorsement, and a study visa each allow only the work they name. A permanent residence permit, a current Zimbabwean or Lesotho exemption permit, and a relative's visa held by the parent described in *Rayment* are the instruments whose sources do not tie the work to one employer.

# Anyone may browse Artisans; only Accounts post and hire

A Visitor may browse Artisan Profiles by Service Category and Region and open any profile, with its badges, work photos, Regions, rating, and Reviews, but no direct contact. Everything else (posting, inviting, Quoting, hiring, and Jobs) needs a signed-in Account. Public profiles let an Artisan share a link and let search engines find them. A landing page alone was rejected because a Client could not judge the marketplace before signing up, and an Artisan's best marketing is a link to their profile.

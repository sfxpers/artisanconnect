# The Artisan Fee is on the whole Quote, and steps down by Completed Engagements

The brief charged the Artisan Fee on Labour only, at 10, 7, and 4 percent for the first, second, and later paid Engagement in a Client Relationship. The Artisan writes the Materials amount, and nobody checks it, so Labour of R1 and Materials of R9,999 would have paid a fee of ten cents. The fee is on the whole accepted Quote, Labour and Materials together, and the Quote still shows the split for the Client to read. The rate is set at Payment by the Completed Engagements already in that Client Relationship: none, one, or two or more. A paid Engagement that ends in a Cancellation or a full Return does not count, because otherwise two small paid-and-returned Jobs would buy the lowest rate. Materials receipts were rejected because an Admin would then judge every Quote. A cap on the Materials share was rejected because any line drawn is arbitrary and still leaves a discount under it.

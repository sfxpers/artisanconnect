# Everything sent is read and checked, and there is no video

Every Job, Quote, Artisan Profile, Conversation message, and Review is checked before anyone sees it. Photos are read by OCR, voice notes are turned into text, and PDFs have their text extracted, so everything becomes text. A pattern check then refuses a phone number, an email address, a link, or a bank account number at once. Workers AI then reads the text for what patterns miss: spelled-out numbers, social handles, and asks to deal or pay off the platform. It also spots a contact card or contact screenshot in a photo. Before Payment only text and photos may be sent, and contact is refused. After Payment voice notes and PDFs are allowed too, contact may be swapped, and only payment details and asks to pay off the platform are refused. A sure hit is refused with the reason; an unsure one is Held for the Admin, who releases or refuses it (ADR 0020). The same reading also looks for threats, slurs, and harassment. A refusal is not Leaving or a warning, so a wrong AI call costs a rewrite, never a sanction; Leaving is still found by the Admin from a Report. Video is not allowed anywhere, because it cannot be read cheaply. Patterns alone were rejected because a spelled-out number or "pay me cash, skip the fee" would pass; AI alone was rejected because a clear number should be refused instantly, free, and the same way every time.

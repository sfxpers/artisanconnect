# A Review exists only once the Engagement is Completed

The brief let either party Review any paid Engagement, and it published a disputed one after resolution with a mark. A Review exists only once the Engagement is Completed, one from each party. A Cancellation and a full Return have none. It stays unread by the other until both have submitted or fourteen times 24 hours have passed, whichever is first. A missed side cannot write later. There is no Dispute mark. Waiting until Completed already keeps an unresolved claim out of the average, and a mark would be the platform judging the work.

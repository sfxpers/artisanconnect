# Hiring is paying

A Quote carries the Artisan's start date and duration, so sending it is the Artisan's commitment. The Client Hires by paying for it: there is no acceptance step by the Artisan and no unpaid hired state. A different date is agreed in the Conversation and the Artisan revises the Quote before Hire. A flow in which the Client sends dates, the Artisan confirms them, and the Client then pays was rejected: it needs a pending-dates state and an unpaid hired state that closes the Job to other Quotes, and the Client can still walk away at the end of it.

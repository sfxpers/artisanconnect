# An Open Job reaches every eligible Artisan in its Region

An Open Job is shown, and emailed, to every Artisan who is verified for its Service Category, has selected its Region, and is Available for Jobs. It takes Quotes until five arrive, the Client Hires, or 14 days pass. A Region is one of the City of Cape Town's eight Development Management Districts; the Client picks an official suburb and the platform looks up its district. An Artisan selects as many Regions as they will travel to. Rotating batches of ten were rejected: they need a rotation order, tie-breaks, and an expansion clock, and they hide from the Client who was offered the Job, all to ration Jobs among an Artisan pool that is small at launch. Hiding the Job from all but invited Artisans stays available as an Invite-only Job.

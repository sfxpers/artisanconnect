# An Artisan arrives by Region, not by a city boundary

A Job is private. An Artisan sees it only after a Job Match or an Artisan Invitation, and only in a Region they have selected. Launch opens the City of Cape Town's eight Development Management Districts as its Regions, as the City publishes them. Admin may open a later Region; this launch does not. An Artisan selects at most three, and a verified address outside the Region is not a bar. Twelve areas of our own naming were rejected because no official layer has them as an edge, so we would have kept the grouping ourselves. Subcouncils, wards, and suburbs were rejected because their names mean nothing to a Client or their units are too small to fill a batch. A municipal in-or-out list was rejected because Cape Town is the first city, not the only one. Drive-time reach was rejected because the Artisan chooses named areas, not a journey length. Showing the Job and hoping they decline was rejected because the platform filters before the Job is shown.

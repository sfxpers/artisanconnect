# The Artisan Fee steps down once per Client Relationship

The Artisan Fee is taken from each Release, on the whole Milestone amount. It is 10% in a Client Relationship with no Completed Engagement and 5% once there is one. The rate is set at Hire and does not change during the Engagement. A Labour-only fee was rejected because the Artisan writes the split and could call most of the price materials. Three steps (10, 7, 4) were rejected as a rule nobody would remember, and a flat fee was rejected because a cheaper repeat is the reason to keep the next job on the platform.

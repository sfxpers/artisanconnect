# A Job is offered in rotating Batches of ten, inside its Region

A Job is private. The platform offers it to a Batch of up to ten eligible Artisans (verified for its Service Category, in its Region, and Available for Jobs), choosing those offered a Job least recently, and offers another Batch every 24 hours while it has fewer than five Quotes. The Client never sees a Batch. A Region is one of the City of Cape Town's eight Development Management Districts, derived from the Client's suburb, and an Artisan selects up to three. Showing every Job to every eligible Artisan was rejected because the fastest five would win every Job and the same Artisans would see every Client's home; rating-first order was rejected because newcomers would never get a look. An Invite-only Job skips Batches entirely.

# A Job Match batch rotates by last offer

The brief ranked Artisans by relevance, verification, reliability, rating, availability, and activity. The Client never sees who was offered the Job, and the Client chooses later among Quotes, where rating is already visible. A stable merit list would hand the same Artisans every first look. The ten are whoever may Quote and was least recently offered a Job Match, of any Service Category. A published Review in that category is only a tie-break. Paid placement, a Reliability Record, an optional badge, and a response rate do not move an Artisan. Browse is a separate order, inside one Service Category, and it may use only what the profile shows: Available for Jobs, then the published average and count, then public name. The Client can see that order and cannot see the rotation.

# A Conversation is not private from the platform

The Admin may read a Conversation and may not write in it. The Admin opens one only from a Report, a Dispute, or a Chargeback on that Job, every read is logged, and there is no search of every Conversation. Both parties are told it may be read before their first message. End-to-end privacy was rejected because Leaving and a Dispute would then be unjudgeable.

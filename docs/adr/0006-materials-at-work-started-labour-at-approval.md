# Materials are released at Work started, Labour at Approval

The Client pays the whole Quote at Hire. Its Materials are released when work starts on site, so the Artisan is not out of pocket for the goods, and its Labour is released when the Client approves the Completion or seven days pass without an answer. The Client may ask for a fix, which restarts the seven days, or Dispute a named part of the Labour. Releasing everything at Completion was rejected because South African Artisans would have to fund all materials themselves. Several Artisan-defined milestones were rejected as more structure than a repair or installation Job needs. A free-choice deposit was rejected because the Materials line already says what the money is for. Work started is set by the Client, or by the Artisan when the Client does not answer within 24 hours, so no Client can hold the Materials hostage.

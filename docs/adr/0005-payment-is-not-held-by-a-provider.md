# A Payment is not held by a provider

No checked South African provider holds the Client's Payment and later pays it to the Artisan's bank account. The product records a Payment, then each Release, then a Payout. The adapter collects into the platform's processor relationship and pays the Artisan later from that relationship, or from a float the platform funds. The product does not record that float. Paying the Artisan when the Client pays was rejected because that is before the work. Waiting for a provider that can hold the money was rejected because none of the checked docs show one.

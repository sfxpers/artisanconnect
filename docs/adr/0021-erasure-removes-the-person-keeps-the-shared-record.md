# Erasure removes the person and keeps the shared record

Erasure, asked for in a Data request, anonymises a Closed Account; asking for it closes the Account first, as erasure is only of a Closed one, and the Admin erases it only once no money is still owed to it. What names the person goes: its names (it shows as "An Account", and is "Erased Account" to the Admin), its Email, which another sign-up may then take, its password and sessions, the devices and IPs it was seen on, its Notices, its own Support messages, its Profile, its stored files (identity documents, photos), and any export it was sent. What is also another party's or the platform's record stays as written: its Jobs, Quotes, Conversations, and Engagements, which the other party keeps; its money records, with the Payout account they were paid to; its Reviews, shown without the name; its warnings and Suspensions; its Identity Number, so that one still holds at most one Artisan Account; and the Admin's own records (queue items, decisions, Signals, and the audit log). Deleting the Account outright was rejected because the other party's Engagement, the ledger, and Reviews hang on it. Erasing what the other party also holds, such as a Conversation or a Job's address, was rejected because a delivered message is never changed (ADR `0010`) and the work it records may still be disputed or charged back. Freeing the Identity Number was rejected because a Suspended Artisan could then erase itself and verify a new Account. An erased Account is never reopened.

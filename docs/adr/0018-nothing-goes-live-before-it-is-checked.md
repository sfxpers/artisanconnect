# Nothing goes live before it is checked, and what cannot be checked cheaply is not allowed before Payment

Defence runs in three layers, in order: automatic checks before anything goes live, then a person's approval for what automation cannot judge, then reports for what both missed. Before Payment a Conversation, a Job, a Quote attachment, and an Artisan Profile take only text, images, PDFs, and voice notes. Each is turned into text (metadata stripped, OCR, text extraction, speech-to-text) and run through the one text detector, and a check that fails or cannot run refuses the send. Video and every other file type are refused before Payment and allowed in a Conversation after it, when the withheld facts may be said. A check passes or refuses and never holds an item for a person. Workers AI is used only to turn media into text, never to judge it. Leaving media to reports was rejected because a report acts after people have seen it, and removing the channel was cheaper than building a video check this stack cannot run. Approving every Job was rejected because a Job is private to its matched Artisans and a queue in front of it costs more than it prevents. This amends ADR 0016 and the stack decision's "Workers AI stays unused".

# An Updated Quote only raises the price

When the site differs from the Job, the Artisan proposes an Updated Quote before Completion. Neither Labour nor Materials may go down, and it applies only when the Client pays the difference plus its Protection Fee; rejected, the Hired Quote stands. A lower price is the Artisan's Refund, which needs no agreement. Letting an Updated Quote lower the price was rejected because a lowered Payment would need a refund path through the Updated Quote as well as the Refund.

# The Artisan Fee is on the whole Quote and steps down once

The Artisan Fee is taken from each Release, Materials and Labour alike: 10% in a Client Relationship with no Completed Engagement and 5% once there is one, set at Hire. A Labour-only fee was rejected because the Artisan writes the split and could call most of the price Materials. Three steps were rejected as a rule nobody would remember, and a flat fee because a cheaper repeat is the reason to keep the next Job on the platform.

# A Hire Again Job is not offered as a Job Match

A posted Job that passes the checks can be matched at once. Hire Again is the Client's act of opening a new Job addressed only to an Artisan with whom the Client has a Completed Engagement. That Job is an Artisan Invitation and does not enter a Job Match batch. An ordinary Job still does. The Client already has Invite to a Job for other Quotes. The fee is the Artisan Fee already decided for that Client Relationship, not a separate rate.
